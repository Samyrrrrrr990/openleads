## r/coldemail

Read the rules first. They don't allow promo posts on most days, so this is written
as a question with the tool mentioned once. If the rules say no self-promotion at
all, skip this sub today.

**Title:** Do you approve every first email by hand, or let sequences send on their own?

**Body:**

I've gone back and forth on this. When I let a sequence send the first email
unattended, I'd find out a week later that half the list was wrong (an agency
instead of a clinic, someone who had already replied to a different thread).

So in the open source lead tool I work on (OpenLeads, free, runs locally) I changed
the default: first emails now wait in a queue until I say yes, and follow-ups only
go once the first one was approved. In the morning I get one screen with replies
waiting on me, emails to approve and calls due, and I clear it in about ten minutes.

It's slower on paper. My reply rate went up because the bad matches never go out.

Curious how others here handle it. Do you review every first email, spot check a
sample, or trust the list and let it run?

## LinkedIn

I shipped OpenLeads 5.3 today. It's a free, open source alternative to Apollo that
runs on your own laptop.

The change I'm happiest with is small. You type what you want:

openleads "find 30 rust devs in Berlin and email them about our hiring tool"

and it shows you the plan before anything happens: who it will look for, what it
will write, and that the emails will wait for your approval. Say yes and it runs.

Every morning, `openleads today` shows replies waiting on you, emails to approve and
tasks due, with one key to approve, skip or snooze each one. The same screen is the
home page of the dashboard, and Claude or Cursor can go through it with you over MCP.

It's AGPL and free to use. If your company wants to use it in a product or a hosted
service, email me and we'll sort out terms.
