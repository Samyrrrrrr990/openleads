# Product Hunt

**Name:** OpenLeads
**Tagline (60):** Free lead finder that tells you which emails are guesses
**Topics:** Sales, Open Source, Developer Tools, Artificial Intelligence

**Description:**
Type who you want ("dentists in Austin", "fintech founders") and OpenLeads finds the
businesses and people from public data, then labels each email: found, pattern, or
guessed. It runs on your machine, needs no API keys, and can send on a schedule
within safe daily limits. It also works inside Claude and Cursor as an MCP server.
Open source (AGPL).
