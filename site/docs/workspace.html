The workspace: people, companies, lists, tasks and deals

Everything you find, import or add lands in one local workspace — the CRM side of OpenLeads. It's the same data in the dashboard (openleads web), the terminal (openleads people, openleads tasks, …) and your AI assistant (the MCP workspace tools). It lives in ~/.openleads/openleads.db, a SQLite file on your machine.

openleads demo          # try it first on an invented workspace (your data is untouched)

#People

Every contact has the fields you'd expect from Apollo — name, title, company, email with its tier (safe / risky) and evidence (found / pattern / guessed), phone, LinkedIn, location — plus what OpenLeads works out for you:

  • Seniority (owner, founder, C-suite, partner, VP, head, director, manager, senior, entry) and department (sales, marketing, engineering, …) from the title.
  • Fit score, 0–100 with an A–D grade, from your ideal-customer model (see ICP scoring).
  • Stage, tags, lists, notes, tasks, deals, the sequence they're in, and a timeline of everything that happened.

The People page is a filterable table: facets with live counts (stage, email status, evidence, ICP grade, seniority, department, industry, company size, tech stack, location, tags, lists, sequence, email activity, last contacted, phone, LinkedIn), free-text search, sortable and configurable columns, and bulk actions on a selection or on every row matching the filters — add to a list, add to a sequence, set stage, tag, assign an owner, export, write emails, mark do-not-contact or delete. Save any filter combination as a saved search.

openleads people                                   # most recently updated
openleads people dental --fit A --never-contacted
openleads people --stage interested --json
openleads people show jane@acme.com                # the full record
openleads people stage jane@acme.com --to meeting
openleads people tag jane@acme.com --add vip,q4
openleads people note jane@acme.com "Wants pricing for 20 seats"

#Stages

StageMoves here automatically when…
Newthe contact is added
Approachingthe first email is sent
Replieda reply arrives (with IMAP connected)
Interested · Meeting booked · Customeryou set it (or a deal is won → Customer)
Not interested · Unresponsiveyou set it, or a sequence finishes with no reply (Unresponsive)
Do not contactthey unsubscribe, or you mark them
Bad datathe email bounces

Automatic moves never drag a contact backwards: a follow-up sent to someone you marked Interested leaves them Interested. A reply, bounce or unsubscribe also takes the person out of any active sequence.

#Companies

Companies (accounts) are created from your contacts' domains and filled in from their homepage on demand: name, description, industry, headcount band, founded year, phone, address, social profiles and the tech stack their site runs (100+ technologies across 30 categories — HubSpot, Shopify, Stripe, Intercom, WordPress, …). Filter companies by technology, industry, size, location and stage, and open one to see its people, deals, tasks and notes.

openleads companies --tech HubSpot
openleads companies show acme.com
openleads company acme.com            # live lookup of any domain, not just saved ones

Account stages: prospect, engaged, opportunity, customer, churned, disqualified.

#Lists and tags

Lists are static groups of people (or companies) you build from searches, selections and imports, then work as a unit: open one in the People table, put it in a sequence, export it. Tags are free-form labels on people.

openleads lists create "Q4 dentists"
openleads lists add "Q4 dentists" --where dental          # everyone matching a search
openleads lists add "Q4 dentists" jane@acme.com bob@beta.io
openleads lists show "Q4 dentists"

#Importing a CSV

Import & enrich → Import CSV (or openleads import) takes an export from Apollo, HubSpot, Salesforce, LinkedIn Sales Navigator or any spreadsheet. Columns map themselves from 100+ header spellings; you can change any of them and see real values under each. Existing contacts are matched by email and updated without blanking anything. Rows with a name and a company but no email can go straight to the email finder.

openleads import apollo-export.csv --preview            # just show the mapping
openleads import apollo-export.csv --list "Apollo import" --tags apollo \
    --missing-out missing.csv
openleads enrich missing.csv                             # find the emails it didn't have

#ICP scoring

Describe your ideal customer as points in Settings → ICP scoring: seniority, department, industry, company size, technologies, title keywords, location and reachability (verified email, phone, LinkedIn). Every contact gets a 0–100 fit score — A 75+, B 55+, C 35+, D below — and the editor previews how many grades would change and who the best matches are before you save. Each contact's record explains its score line by line.

#Tasks

Calls, emails, LinkedIn touches and to-dos. Sequence steps that need a human become tasks on their due date; completing one moves that contact on to their next step. The Tasks page groups work into Overdue, Today, Upcoming and No date, and Start queue works through everything due one at a time, with the contact's details, a note box, and keyboard shortcuts (Enter done, S skip, Z snooze).

openleads tasks --bucket today
openleads tasks add "Call Jane about pricing" --contact jane@acme.com --kind call --due tomorrow
openleads tasks done 12
openleads tasks snooze 13 --due +3d

#Deals

A drag-and-drop pipeline: Qualify (10%), Meeting (25%), Proposal (50%), Negotiation (75%), Won, Lost. The board shows each stage's value and probability-weighted value; the header shows open pipeline, weighted forecast, won and win rate. Winning a deal marks its contact and company as customers.

openleads deals
openleads deals add "Acme — annual plan" --amount 12k --contact jane@acme.com
openleads deals move 3 proposal
openleads deals won 3

#Activity and data health

Activity is the workspace-wide feed — sends, replies, stage changes, notes, tasks, deal moves, list and sequence changes, searches and imports — grouped by day and filterable by what happened and by record type.

Data health lists what to fix before you send: unverified and guessed emails, bounces, missing titles, phones and LinkedIn, untouched contacts and unprofiled companies, each one click from the matching People filter. It also finds duplicate people (the same person at the same company under two addresses) so you can pick the record to keep and merge — tags, lists, notes, tasks, deals, sequences and history move onto it.

openleads stats            # the workspace at a glance
openleads dedupe           # list duplicates
openleads dedupe --merge   # merge each group into its best record

#Exporting and backups

Export any selection or filter from People as CSV, JSON, or in Apollo or HubSpot import format, or push it to Google Sheets, Notion, Airtable or a webhook. From the terminal:

openleads lists export "Q4 dentists" -f hubspot -o q4.csv
openleads find "…" -f apollo
openleads export

Settings → Data & backup (or openleads backup) takes a consistent snapshot of the whole workspace — safe while sequences are running — into ~/.openleads/backups/. To restore, stop OpenLeads and copy a backup over ~/.openleads/openleads.db.
