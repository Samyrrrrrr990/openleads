Licensing

OpenLeads is open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later, with a commercial license for teams that can't meet the AGPL's terms. Copyright © 2026 Samyar Shafiee.

#Do I need to pay?

Almost certainly not.

What you're doingWhat you need
Running OpenLeads to find leads and send email, for yourself or your businessNothing. Free.
Using it inside your company, agency or startupNothing. Free.
Changing the code for your own useNothing, as long as you don't distribute it or offer it to others over a network.
Shipping a modified version, or letting others use it over a network (a hosted tool, SaaS, an API)Publish your full source under the AGPL, or buy a commercial license.
Embedding OpenLeads in a closed-source productA commercial license.

The leads you find and the emails you write are yours. The AGPL covers the OpenLeads code, not your data or your output.

#Commercial license

Email samyar.shafiee2009@gmail.com with your company, how you'll use OpenLeads, and rough scale. Small teams and early startups usually get a nominal fee. Details: COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md.

#Earlier versions

Versions up to and including 4.0.1 were released under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 and keep it. Everything from 4.5.0 on is AGPL.

Pull requests are licensed under the AGPL, and you agree they may also ship in commercially licensed versions. That's what makes the dual license work. You keep your copyright.
