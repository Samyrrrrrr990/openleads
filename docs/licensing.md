## Do I need to pay?

Almost certainly not.

| What you're doing | What you need |
|---|---|
| Running OpenLeads to find leads and send email, for yourself or your business | Nothing. Free. |
| Using it inside your company, agency or startup | Nothing. Free. |
| Changing the code for your own use | Nothing, as long as you don't distribute it or offer it to others over a network. |
| Shipping a modified version, or letting others use it over a network (a hosted tool, SaaS, an API) | Publish your full source under the AGPL, **or** buy a commercial license. |
| Embedding OpenLeads in a closed-source product | A commercial license. |

The leads you find and the emails you write are yours. The AGPL covers the
OpenLeads code, not your data or your output.
